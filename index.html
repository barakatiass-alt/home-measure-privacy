Privacy policy

Home Measure has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your homes stay on your iPhone.

On your iPhone

  • Camera and LiDAR data are processed on your iPhone by Apple's RoomPlan framework.
  • Homes, rooms, measurements, floor plans and reports are saved only in the app's own storage on your iPhone.
  • If "Save photos for realistic 3D" is on (Settings, on by default), the app keeps some camera images from each scan on your iPhone to colour the 3D model with your real walls and floors. The photo pass for the photoreal model saves more of them. They are deleted with the home. Turn the setting off to keep no images.
  • To line up rooms you scan at different times, the app keeps an AR world map of the home (feature points, no photos) on your iPhone for up to 12 hours. It is deleted with the home.
  • The photoreal trainer and the 3D viewer are part of the app and run on your iPhone. Your photos and models stay on the iPhone and are not uploaded. On a computer, the trainer page runs in your browser and uploads nothing either.
  • Your photo library is opened only when you choose a logo for your reports. Only the image you pick is used.
  • The app does not use location, microphone, contacts or notifications.

When you share

  • A report, floor plan, 3D file or listing text leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself through the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you choose.
  • Tour links. If you create a 3D tour link, the app asks first, then uploads the home's 3D model (also as a file for "View in AR" on iPhones, with a picture of it for link previews, and the PDF report with its listing text; with photo textures of the walls and floors when realistic 3D is on, and the photoreal model when you trained one) to our tour service, which stores it with Cloudflare. Anyone with the link can view the tour. On Pro, your brand name, contact line and logo go with the tour when you set them in Settings. The link uses a random 20-character address that cannot be guessed, and tour pages ask search engines not to list them. Tours and their files are deleted after 12 months.
  • To check your plan, the app sends the tour service the App Store transaction ID of your purchase and a random id for the home. The service asks Apple whether the purchase is valid and keeps a count of the homes shared each month, deleted after 40 days. If you use a single export, it keeps a note that that purchase was used. None of this includes your name, Apple ID or payment details.
  • Send scan data (Settings) makes one file with all your homes (names, addresses, sizes) and their raw scans, without photos, for checking a problem or moving to a new iPhone. The app warns you first, and you choose where it goes.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare runs the tour service and stores tour links. Like any web server, it sees the IP address of the app and of people who open a tour; we do not keep logs of them.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site, the tour viewer and the trainer page.
  • Expo delivers app updates: at launch the app asks whether a newer version of its code exists, sending the app version and a random install id. No homes or personal details are sent.
  • Apple handles purchases and checks them for the tour service.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Deleting your data

Delete a room or home inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. Tour links expire after 12 months. To remove one sooner, open the home, Share, Delete tour link; deleting the home deletes its tour too (if the iPhone is offline, the app finishes the delete the next time it opens). For a link made by an older version, contact us with the link.

Support

Which iPhones can scan?

iPhones with a LiDAR sensor: iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, on iOS 17 or later. On other iPhones, use "Add a room" to type in sizes. The report works the same way. Training the photoreal model on the iPhone needs iOS 26.

How accurate is it?

Scanned rooms are usually within 1 to 2 percent, and the report shows a likely range. Rooms typed in by hand are less precise and show Medium confidence. It is a practical measurement of net internal area, not a legal or land survey.

Tips for a good scan

Turn on the lights, walk slowly along the walls, point at every corner, and keep doors open between rooms when you scan the whole home in one go.

Photoreal model

After the scan, do the photo pass and follow the ring in the corner until it fills. Then train with the iPhone plugged in and the app open. It pauses by itself if the iPhone gets hot.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchase back?

Open Settings in the app, then your plan, and tap Restore purchases with the same Apple ID.

Can I use another language?

Yes. The app follows your iPhone's language (English, Hebrew, French, Spanish, Arabic, Italian or Thai), or pick one in Settings, App language. With full access, reports come in those languages too (Settings, Report language). Tour links open in the language of whoever views them.

How do I cancel the monthly or yearly plan?

iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Home Measure, Cancel subscription.
