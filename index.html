Privacy policy

Home Measure has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. Your homes stay on your iPhone.

On your iPhone

  • Camera and LiDAR data are processed on your iPhone by Apple's RoomPlan framework.
  • Homes, rooms, measurements, floor plans and reports are saved only in the app's own storage on your iPhone.
  • If "Save photos for realistic 3D" is on (Settings, on by default), the app keeps some camera images from each scan on your iPhone to colour the 3D model with your real walls and floors. The photo pass for the photoreal model saves more of them. They are deleted with the home. Turn the setting off to keep no images.
  • To line up rooms you scan at different times, the app keeps an AR world map of the home (feature points, no photos) on your iPhone for up to 12 hours. It is deleted with the home.
  • Training and viewing the photoreal model inside the app use our web pages (the trainer and the 3D tour on this site), loaded in the app. Your photos and models are handed to them on the iPhone and are not uploaded. On a computer, the trainer page runs in your browser and uploads nothing either.
  • Your photo library is opened only when you choose a logo for your reports (Pro). Only the image you pick is used.
  • The app does not use location, microphone, contacts or notifications.

When you share

  • A report, floor plan, 3D file or listing text leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself through the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you choose.
  • Tour links. If you create a 3D tour link, the app asks first, then uploads the home's 3D model (with photo textures of the walls and floors when realistic 3D is on, and the photoreal model when you trained one) to our tour service, which stores it with Cloudflare. Anyone with the link can view the tour. The link uses a random 20-character address that cannot be guessed. Tours and their files are deleted after 12 months.
  • To check your plan, the app sends the tour service the App Store transaction ID of your purchase and a random id for the home. The service asks Apple whether the purchase is valid and keeps a count of the homes shared each month, deleted after 40 days. If you use a single export, it keeps a note that that purchase was used. None of this includes your name, Apple ID or payment details.

Purchases

Payments are handled by Apple. We never see your payment details or your Apple ID.

Deleting your data

Delete a room or home inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored on your iPhone. Tour links expire after 12 months; to remove one sooner, contact us with the link.

Support

Which iPhones can scan?

iPhones with a LiDAR sensor: iPhone 12 Pro and later Pro models, on iOS 17 or later. On other iPhones, use "Add a room" to type in sizes. The report works the same way. Training the photoreal model on the iPhone needs iOS 26.

How accurate is it?

Scanned rooms are usually within 1 to 2 percent, and the report shows a likely range. Rooms typed in by hand are less precise and show Medium confidence. It is a practical measurement of net internal area, not a legal or land survey.

Tips for a good scan

Turn on the lights, walk slowly along the walls, point at every corner, and keep doors open between rooms when you scan the whole home in one go.

Photoreal model

After the scan, do the photo pass and follow the ring in the corner until it fills. Then train with the iPhone plugged in and the app open. It pauses by itself if the iPhone gets hot.

I changed phones. How do I get my purchase back?

Open Settings in the app, then your plan, and tap Restore purchases with the same Apple ID.

How do I cancel Plus or Pro?

iPhone Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Home Measure, Cancel subscription.
