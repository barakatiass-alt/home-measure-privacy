Home Measure

Privacy policy and support. Last updated 20 October 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing. Home Measure has no accounts, no servers and no analytics.

  • Camera and LiDAR data are processed on your iPhone by Apple's RoomPlan framework.
  • If “Save photos for realistic 3D” is on (Settings, on by default), the app keeps some camera images from each scan on your iPhone to colour the 3D model with your real walls and floors. They never leave your iPhone unless you share the 3D model yourself, and they are deleted with the home. Turn the setting off to keep no images.
  • Homes, rooms, measurements, floor plans and reports are saved only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage.
  • To line up rooms you scan at different times, the app keeps an AR world map of the home (feature points, no photos) on your iPhone for up to 12 hours. It is deleted with the home.
  • The app does not use location, your photo library, microphone, contacts or notifications.

Sharing

A report, 3D model or listing text leaves your iPhone only when you share it yourself through the iOS share sheet, to the app or person you choose.

Deleting your data

Delete a room or home inside the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Support

How accurate is it? Scanned rooms are typically within 1 to 2 percent. Rooms typed in by hand are less precise and show Medium confidence. This is a practical measurement of net internal area, not a legal or land survey.

My iPhone has no LiDAR. Use "Add a room" to type in width and depth. The report works the same way.

Tips for a good scan: turn on the lights, walk slowly along the walls, point at every corner, then tap Finish room.
